## Engineering principles

- Enforce authorization in trusted systems, not in prompts or model-generated decisions.
- Make security controls repeatable, testable, and automated.
- Design tenant boundaries and least privilege into the architecture.
- Prioritize remediation by exploitability, exposure, and business impact.
- Measure security outcomes and make ownership clear.
